How to Revise for GCSE (Actually Works)

Most students revise the wrong way. They reread notes and hope it sticks. It doesn’t.

Step 1: Stop passive revision

Reading notes is not enough. You need active recall and practice questions.

Step 2: Use a clear plan

If you don’t know what to revise each day, you waste time deciding.

Create your GCSE revision plan here

Step 3: Be consistent

Consistency beats intensity. Study a little every day.

Step 4: Test yourself

Use past papers and mark your answers.

Step 5: Fix weak areas

Focus more time on topics you struggle with.
