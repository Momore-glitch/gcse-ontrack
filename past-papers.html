A quick paper-to-progress routine

  1. Attempt: use the question paper without notes and keep to the time limit.
  2. Mark: use the official mark scheme and be honest about lost marks.
  3. Diagnose: label each lost mark as a knowledge gap, exam-technique issue or careless error.
  4. Act: choose one weak topic, use a relevant video or resource, then try new questions.
  5. Record: Premium users can log paper scores, resources used and the next action in the evidence tracker.
