Turn a paper or lesson into progress

  1. Attempt: answer questions before looking at the mark scheme.
  2. Mark: use the official mark scheme and record your score honestly.
  3. Find the gap: note the topic or exam skill that cost you marks.
  4. Practise: watch a focused explanation, then answer fresh questions without notes.
  5. Keep the next action: save useful links in My Resources or plan the next session.
