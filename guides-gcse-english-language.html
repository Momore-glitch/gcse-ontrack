How to Revise English Language for GCSE

GCSE English Language is about understanding questions, analysing texts, and practising your writing skills.

Step 1: Understand the question types

Learn what each question is asking. This is key to gaining marks.

Step 2: Practise analysing texts

Read extracts and explain language, structure, and effects clearly.

Step 3: Improve your writing

Practise writing descriptions, narratives, and responses under timed conditions.
